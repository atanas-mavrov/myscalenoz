# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

- Live measurement over Bluetooth with MyKronoz / Yolanda `QN-Scale` scales;
  body composition (14 values) computed on the phone.
- Multiple users, first-start setup and an explanation of why each detail is
  needed.
- Home screen with trends and recent measurements; statistics with line, bar
  and scatter charts; full measurement history.
- Explanations for every value.
- Backup and restore (JSON) with a documented format; CSV export.
- Delete one user's data or all data, with confirmation.
- Saved scales with custom names.
- Chime when the measurement is done.
- English, Bulgarian, German, Spanish and French; light and dark theme.
